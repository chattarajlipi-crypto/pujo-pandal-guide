Pujo Pandal Guide

Find a Durga Puja pandal in West Bengal, see the nearest metro, train station and bus stop, and get a short route to walk in. Anyone can add a pandal below.

Add a pandal

Know a pandal that is missing? Add its name, area and how to get there. It appears in the list for everyone.

Walking times and stops are approximate. Roads close and buses are diverted around big pandals during Puja, and Kolkata Metro usually adds late-night services, so confirm timings on the day. Themes change every year; the starter list says what each pandal is known for, and visitors can add this year's theme.
